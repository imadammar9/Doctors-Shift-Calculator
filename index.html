How the numbers are calculated
  1. Patients per shift = patients per day × the shift's share (× the what-if change).
  2. The calculator uses the Erlang C queueing model, the standard for call centres and telemedicine queues, to estimate waiting times for a given number of doctors.
  3. Doctors per shift: the smallest number that meets your service level and stays under the occupancy limit, including breaks. Patients are assumed to arrive evenly through the 8 hours. Breaks are staggered, so while one doctor is on a break the others cover. A doctor working alone takes breaks between patients.
  4. Doctor cost: per shift or per hour = doctors × pay (shift C gets the night premium); per consultation = patients × pay; monthly salary = headcount × salary. A month = 30.4 days × clinic days ÷ 7. Paid waiting time = doctor cost × the share of working time doctors are not with a patient.
  5. Headcount = doctors per day × clinic days ÷ shifts per doctor per week ÷ (1 − absence allowance), rounded up.
  6. The result depends most on minutes per patient. Use your real average consultation time, including notes.
